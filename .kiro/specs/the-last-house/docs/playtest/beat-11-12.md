# Playtest checklist: Beats 11-12 (final ritual, final choice, both endings)

Greybox build. Coordinates are metres in the Last House scene (x east, z south; the ritual chamber is at z -30..-6). Controls: WASD move, mouse look, E interact, HOLD E on an anchor, hold E (1 s) skips a skippable cinematic, Esc pause.

## Start points (dev builds only)

- `?start=ritual`: standing in the ritual chamber (0, -12), everything up to Sequence 6 done (`seq6_done`), the three anchor doors are open, the seal is ready to be woken. Use this for all of Beat 11.
- `?start=ritual-choice`: same place, but the ritual is already finished (all three anchors restored). Use this for Beat 12 and both endings.
- A normal new game reaches the same place by playing Beats 1-10.
- To test "Continue is hidden", play an ending to the end, then check the main menu. Use a normal browser profile (the save lives in browser storage); "Reset progress" in Settings clears it afterwards.

## Beat 11: the ritual

1. Stand at the start (0, -12). Walk to the seal in the middle of the chamber, about (0, -15), and look at it.
   - You should see: a grey stone drum with a faint purple glow, and the prompt "E  Wake the seal".
   - You should NOT see: any smoke warning yet, or any anchor prompt.
2. Press E.
   - Hear/see: Elena says "Three anchors. Gran, show me how." About 3 s later the Angel says "North wants force. East wants patience. West wants mercy. Go."
   - Expected result in plain words: the ritual is now active. The seal prompt changes to "E  Restore the anchors". Pressing E on it again only repeats the Angel's line.
   - You should NOT see: a cutscene, or lose control.
3. Wait about 4 s without moving.
   - You should see: a text bar near the top of the screen "≈ The smoke gathers…" for about 1 s, then "≈ The smoke strikes, and passes" for about half a second. Once, near the start, you also hear the Smoke: "Little keeper. Little lock."
   - Expected result: the strikes are only a warning. Your health never drops (placeholder: the real rings, pools and wraiths come later). They come a little faster after each restored anchor (every 4 s, then 3.5 s, then 3 s) and stop for good when the last anchor is restored. After the second anchor the Smoke may say "Open. Open."
4. Walk out of the chamber through any of the three gaps in its walls (3 m wide): north at (0, -30), east at (12, -18), west at (-12, -18). Each leads to a small room with one anchor. You can do them in any order.
   - You should see: the gaps are open (no brown door blocks). Each room has one grey box on the floor, the anchor, in its middle: north (0, -40), east (20, -18), west (-20, -18).
5. NORTH anchor (hold 3 s). Stand within 2 m of it. The prompt reads "E  Restore the north anchor (hold)".
   - This anchor needs Arcane Bolt as your selected spell (the HUD shows "✦ Arcane Bolt", which is the default). It stands in for breaking the smoke shell first.
   - Press and HOLD E.
   - You should see: a bar in the lower middle of the screen "Keep holding E" that fills over 3 s. At the end the anchor box disappears and the north flag is set.
   - Release E halfway: the bar vanishes and the progress is lost. Press and hold again: it starts from empty.
   - Step more than about 3 m away while holding: the bar vanishes and the progress is lost.
   - You should NOT see: the anchor finish if you let go early.
6. FIRST anchor of the three (whichever you do first), the moment it finishes:
   - You should see: a black panel in the middle labelled "cin_felix_anchor / cinematic placeholder" for 15 s. You cannot move. (Real clip later: Felix's spirit beside the seal encouraging Elena.)
   - You can skip it: hold E for 1 s; a ring fills.
   - Expected result in plain words: Felix's third appearance is done (`felix3_done`). It plays once only: the second and third anchors have no clip.
   - You should NOT see: a second clip after the other anchors, or anything that locks the strikes forever.
7. EAST anchor (hold 6 s). Same as step 5 but 6 s and no spell is needed. The strikes keep appearing while you hold; they do nothing. (Ward and the real strikes are not in yet.)
8. WEST anchor (hold 4 s). Same as step 5 but 4 s. (Purify channel and wraiths are not in yet.)
9. Try a different order on another run (for example west, north, east). All six orders must work.
10. After the third anchor:
    - Hear/see: Elena says "It's quiet. It's stopped." The smoke warnings stop for good.
    - Expected result in plain words: the ritual is complete (`anchors_all`). The seal prompt is now "E  Decide the seal".
11. Dying (only if a future enemy hurts you): you respawn at the ritual chamber centre (0, -12) with full health. The anchors you restored and the active ritual stay done. (Nothing in this build can hurt you, so this is covered by an automatic test; the in-game respawn move is not yet wired to the body, see known placeholders.)

## Beat 12: the final choice (use `?start=ritual-choice`)

1. Walk to the seal (0, -15). The prompt is "E  Decide the seal". Press E.
   - You should see: the game freezes behind a dark panel, the mouse is free, and only the Angel's line with a "Continue" button. The line depends on how you played, so test it at least three times with different play styles: kind ("She chose to keep it. You can too..."), neutral ("This part is yours. I could argue either side beautifully. I won't. Choose."), dark ("Break it. He's right there...").
   - You should NOT see: any number, any hint of which way you "scored", or a timer.
2. Wait a minute on this panel without doing anything.
   - Expected: nothing happens. There is no timer at any step. Esc does nothing here.
3. Click Continue.
   - You should see: exactly two buttons: "Restore the seal and take Felix's place as its keeper (1)" and "Break the seal and pull Felix free (2)". Keys 1 and 2 also work.
4. Click one.
   - You should see: a confirm: the option text, then "This cannot be undone.", a "Yes, I am sure" button and "Back (Esc)".
   - Press Esc (or Back): you return to the two options. Esc at the two-option step does nothing, and Esc never closes the whole panel.
   - Expected: nothing is decided until you press "Yes, I am sure".
5. Press "Yes, I am sure".
   - You should see: the panel closes and a black panel "cin_ending_keeper" (or "cin_ending_broken") for about 25 s. Hold E for 1 s skips it.
   - Expected result in plain words: the ending you picked is the one you get, whatever your earlier choices were, and only one of the two can ever be set.
6. When the clip ends (or you skip it):
   - You should see: the credits screen with the ending card first. Keeper: "The Bloodline Keeper. Every Halloween after, Elena walks to the end of Maple Street and checks the lock. Felix walks with her." Broken: "The Broken Seal. By morning, the fog had reached the next town." Then the credits text and a "Main menu" button.
7. Press "Main menu".
   - You should see: the main menu with New Game, Settings, Credits and NO "Continue" button.
   - Reload the page: still no Continue. (The save is marked finished.) New Game asks before overwriting the finished save.
8. Run the whole of Beat 12 twice more, once per ending, to see both card texts. Keeper and Broken must both be reachable from the same starting state.
9. Quit in the middle: pick an ending, and while the black panel is up press Esc, "Quit to menu", then Continue.
   - Expected: you come back in the ritual chamber (0, -12) with no choice panel; after you click to resume, the ending clip plays again by itself, then the credits. (If it does not, report it.)

## What you should NOT see anywhere in Beats 11-12

- Any scene change away from the Last House before the credits.
- The word "morality", a score or a bar for it.
- Both ending flags set on one save (impossible by rule).
- A way back into the game from the credits.
- Felix hurt on screen. (The clips are placeholders.)

## Known placeholders

- All three cinematics (`cin_felix_anchor`, `cin_ending_keeper`, `cin_ending_broken`) are black text panels with a timer (15 s, 25 s, 25 s) until the generated video clips exist. Their dialogue (Felix's two lines and the ending lines) is not subtitled yet.
- The smoke strike is a harmless text cue. The real ground rings, hazard pools and the wraiths (combat-tuning "The smoke") are not built; the sum of the anchors only changes how often the cue comes.
- The anchors are hold-E stubs: no smoke shell to shoot (the north anchor just needs Arcane Bolt selected), no Ward needed at the east one, no Purify channel or wraiths at the west one. The three 45 s Angel hints (`vo_angel_p6_h_*`) exist as lines; only the north one is used (when you try the north anchor without Arcane Bolt).
- There is no key yet to change the selected spell in game (the default is Arcane Bolt, so the north anchor works).
- The respawn after dying moves the saved position but the first-person body is not yet moved in the view; nothing in this build can kill you.
- Anchors and the seal are plain grey boxes; no glow or particles.
