# Playtest checklist: locket lines (T), spell keys (1/2/3), respawn

Greybox build, Classic mode. T opens the locket once `angel_awake` is set; T or E plays the next line; when none is left T closes. Lines are text plus subtitle (audio later). Lines marked (P) are invented placeholders. The Angel's tone follows your hidden morality, but you never see a number: expect warm ("dear heart"), wry ("my curious one") or cold ("sweet thing") wording from Beat 4 on. Beats 1-3 have one tone only.

Rule that must hold everywhere: the Angel never says something Elena has not learned yet (see "Knowledge" at the end).

## Locket, in story order

1. Beat 1 (home), before the Angel wakes: T does nothing. After the locket opens by itself ("angel_awake"): T gives "Rosalind's granddaughter. You have her chin..." then "The boy's voice came from the street...". While looking for the key, after about 45 s Elena says the pumpkin line; at about 90 s the pumpkin glows; T then plays an Angel nudge about the pumpkin (P).
2. Beat 2 (empty street): "Silence like this is made, not found", then "Walk towards the light at the corner...", plus a "follow the lights" nudge until the sequence ends.
3. Beat 3 (after the streetlight, walking to No. 31): before Felix's spirit appears, a vague "light at the corner" line (P). After it: "The boy's spirit walks without his body..." and "Your gift has teeth now...".
4. Street fox (optional): T is blocked while the fox is hostile; works once it is stunned.
5. Beat 4 (Briggs): T works only while he is stunned. Before the outcome: "Be careful, child. The man you knew may still be in there" (P). After purify/destroy, tone depends on the outcome: kind ("You gave him back to himself"), neutral ("Curious. You hesitated"), dark ("Efficient. The smoke will think twice"); a second line per tone on the next press.
6. Beat 5 (Gartner, then the fuse hunt): T is blocked during the talk. Afterwards one script line per tone (kind "That boy is a victim...", neutral "A voice that does voices...", dark "The smoke picked the softest child...") and a second one (P). If you are stuck on the fuse: about 60 s near House 66 gives "Your neighbour with the garish lights, perhaps?" on T; at about 120 s the No. 31 garage light flickers and T gives a "that light is flickering for a reason" nudge (P).
7. Beat 6 (House 66): "He moves when you blink. Watch the smoke, not the man." during the Watcher fight (only while he is stunned). After the key is taken: one script line per tone about the lane, plus a second (P).
8. Beat 7 (lane and grounds): in the lane a line per tone about the house/window (script + P). Entering the grounds: the hounds line "Hounds. The smoke has run out of neighbours." plays first. At the padlock puzzle (P3), T gives hints in order as you fail: 1st wrong "fond of dates", 3rd wrong "the page in the lane", 5th wrong "the first two dials are glowing", 7th wrong "every dial is glowing".
9. Beat 8 (mirror): before the mirror cinematic an "a mirror in a carriage house" line (P). After it: a line per tone ("He blames himself...", "A lock cracked by a child...", "So the boy broke it. Interesting...") plus a second (P). At the runes: T hints at 30 s (nudge, P), 60 s ("The glass sees what you can't"), 90 s ("There. By your shoulder").
10. Beat 9 (Last House interior): "A cat. The smoke has a sense of humour." first, then a line per tone (roses/locks) plus a second (P). Talking board (P5): after each reset "What does a house that holds something need?", "Someone who keeps it.", "Keeper, child."
11. Beat 10: before the reveal, a "below us the seal waits" line (P). T is blocked during the reveal cinematic. Afterwards a reaction per tone (script) plus a second (P).
12. Beat 11 (ritual): after waking the seal, T first gives the anchor hints in order north ("Break the shell. Your bolt, Elena."), east ("Hold it, and shield yourself..."), west ("Purify it. The light that saves."); an anchor already restored is skipped. Then lines per tone (all P).
13. Beat 12: before the choice panel, the authored line per tone (kind/neutral/dark). T is blocked while the panel is open. More lines per tone follow (P).

Check each time: a different line on every press, no line repeats until the pool is used up, nothing sounds like it knows a later plot fact.

## Spell keys

1. At the start press 1, 2, 3: nothing changes (HUD stays "Arcane Bolt"); nothing is unlocked yet.
2. After the streetlight (Beat 3) Arcane Bolt is unlocked. 1 keeps it selected. 2 and 3 still do nothing.
3. In Beat 4, Ward unlocks at the first scripted hit and Purify at the stun. Press 3: HUD shows Ward. Press 2: HUD shows Purify. Press 1: back to Arcane Bolt. The HUD must always show the selected spell with its glyph and name.
4. Purify (2 + hold LMB) and Destroy (Arcane Bolt, hold LMB) pick the outcome in Beats 4 and 6 as before; the Beat 11 north anchor needs 1 selected, the west anchor 2, the east anchor 3 (see the Beat 11 checklist for what each stub still does).
5. Reload from a save mid-game: all spells you had are still selectable.

## Respawn

1. Let something kill you (Briggs, the Watcher, hounds, the interior cat). The screen should fade/reset and you should stand at the last checkpoint's spawn point in view, not where you died (before this change only the saved position moved).
2. Check three places: after dying in the garden you stand at the garden gate; after dying to the Watcher at the House 66 gate; after dying in the Last House at the entrance hall or, after the board is solved, in the chamber centre.
3. HP and magic are full; flags, restored anchors and spells are unchanged; the selected spell is unchanged.
4. Die twice in a row: you snap to the spawn both times.

## Knowledge (spoilers the Angel must not give early)

- Before the streetlight: no mention of Felix's spirit, the body or the smoke by name.
- Before Briggs is resolved: nothing about neighbours being corrupted or cleansed.
- Before Gartner: nothing about the back lane, the Last House or Gartner's innocence.
- Before the mirror: nothing about the cracked seal.
- Before the reveal: nothing about "two keys" or Felix being a Living Key.
- Before the ritual starts: nothing about the anchors or the keeper's way.
