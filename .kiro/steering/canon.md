# Canon: Facts That Must Never Change

Use this when writing story text, Angel dialogue or LLM prompts.

- **Elena**: mother of Felix. Her magic is inherited through her bloodline. It does NOT come from the Angel.
- **Felix**: age 8, the first Living Key. His untrained magic damaged the seal. His body is trapped in the Last House. His spirit appears three times: beneath a streetlight, through a mirror, near the final seal.
- **Gartner**: Felix's friend. The evil deceived him into bringing Felix to the house. He is completely innocent, never knowingly helps the evil, and feels frightened and guilty. This must never be contradicted, even by LLM output.
- **Grandmother**: a witch who sealed the evil beneath the Last House. The seal is bound to her bloodline and needs two bloodline members to fully open.
- **The evil**: a faceless swirl of black smoke. It cannot escape without both Living Keys. It manipulated events to bring Elena to the house. It is separate from the Angel.
- **The Angel**: lives in the Whispering Locket. Intelligent, theatrical, curious, sometimes manipulative. Not the main villain. Does not know the whole mystery at the start. Personality shifts with Elena's hidden morality.
- **Endings**: exactly two. Good: The Bloodline Keeper. Bad: The Broken Seal. Decided only by the player's final choice.
- **Tone**: atmosphere, suspense and unease over constant jump scares. No gore. Felix is a child in danger: handle with care, no harm shown to him on screen.
