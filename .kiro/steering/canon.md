# Canon: Facts That Must Never Change

Apply to all story text, Angel dialogue, LLM prompts and quest data.

## People
- **Elena Hart**: Felix's mother. Her magic is inherited through her grandmother's bloodline. It does NOT come from the Angel. It awakens during the night.
- **Felix Hart**: age 8, the **first** Living Key. His untrained magic accidentally damaged the seal. His body is trapped in the Last House. His spirit appears exactly three times: (1) beneath the streetlight, (2) in the antique mirror, (3) beside the central seal when Elena restores the first anchor, where he encourages her and reveals the anchors weaken the evil's hold on him.
- **Gartner**: Felix's friend. The evil deceived him into bringing Felix to the Last House; he thought it was harmless. He is completely innocent, never knowingly helps the evil, and feels frightened and guilty. He tells Elena the only way to the Last House is the back lane behind House 66 that he and Felix used.
- **Rosalind Vane**: Elena's grandmother, a witch. She sealed the evil beneath the Last House. The seal is bound to her bloodline and needs two bloodline members to fully open. The Whispering Locket was hers.
- **Mr Briggs** (No. 31): a neighbour corrupted by the smoke. Victim, not villain.
- **The Watcher of House 66** (Mr Corwin): a corrupted resident the evil uses as a lookout. He knows Felix's name and saw Felix pass. He never contradicts Gartner's innocence. He is not a new main quest and not a seventh horror sequence.

## Forces
- **The evil**: faceless, shapeless swirling black smoke. It cannot escape without both Living Keys. It manipulated events to bring Elena to the house. It is separate from the Angel.
- **The Angel**: lives in the Whispering Locket. Intelligent, theatrical, curious, sometimes manipulative. Not the main villain. Does not know the whole mystery at the start; it learns as Elena does. Its personality shifts with Elena's hidden morality.

## Endings (exactly two, decided only by the final choice)
- **The Bloodline Keeper (good)**: Elena uses her inherited blood magic, as the second Living Key, to transfer the seal's responsibility from Felix to herself. Felix is released. The evil stays imprisoned. Elena survives and leaves the house with Felix. Being guardian is a duty to maintain the seal, not being trapped. Bittersweet but hopeful.
- **The Broken Seal (bad)**: Elena breaks the seal to free Felix at once. Felix survives. The evil escapes and spreads beyond Maple Street.

## Tone
Atmosphere, suspense and unease over constant jump scares. No gore. Felix is a child in danger: no harm to him is shown on screen. Corrupted people and animals are victims; purifying them is mercy.
